\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Calibrating by predicted type or type probabilities leaves rare latent types under-covered; the shortest band covering every type and horizon is a dual-weighted posterior mixture, which \gamut{} estimates, with a split variant that adds a finite-sample guarantee.
It removes FLARE's rare-class under-coverage on ZTF, also under a strict split and on a held-out set; over the main benchmarks its bands are \MainWidthGamutSixtyeight{} (68\%) and \MainWidthGamutNinetyfive{} (95\%) times FLARE's, against \MainWidthValidBaselinesSixtyeight{} and \MainWidthValidBaselinesNinetyfive{} for the always-valid baselines. The default has no finite-sample guarantee; C1 adds little on real data.
