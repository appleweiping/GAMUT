\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Calibrating by the predicted type, or by type probabilities, leaves rare latent types under-covered by a conditional covariance that no such reweighting removes.
The shortest band (in scale-weighted length) covering every type and horizon is a dual-weighted posterior mixture; \gamut{} estimates it with three components, and a split variant adds a finite-sample guarantee.
On ZTF it removes the rare-class under-coverage of FLARE's bands at every origin, also under a strict split, and it passes a pre-registered held-out test.
Over the main benchmarks its bands are \MainWidthGamutSixtyeight{} (68\%) and \MainWidthGamutNinetyfive{} (95\%) times FLARE's, against \MainWidthValidBaselinesSixtyeight{} and \MainWidthValidBaselinesNinetyfive{} for the always-valid baselines.
