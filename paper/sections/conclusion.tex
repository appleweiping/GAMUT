\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Calibrating by predicted type or its probabilities under-covers rare latent types; \gamut{} estimates the shortest band covering every type and horizon, a dual-weighted posterior mixture, and a split variant adds a finite-sample guarantee.
It removes FLARE's rare-class under-coverage on ZTF, also under a strict split and on two held-out sets; over the main benchmarks its bands are \MainWidthGamutSixtyeight{} (68\%) and \MainWidthGamutNinetyfive{} (95\%) times FLARE's, against \MainWidthValidBaselinesSixtyeight{} and \MainWidthValidBaselinesNinetyfive{} for always-valid baselines. The default carries no guarantee.
